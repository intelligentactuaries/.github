# Intelligent Actuaries

**An institutional AI lab for actuarial science.**

We research and build AI systems for the actuarial function: agents, models and methodology, evaluated against the regulations our partners file under. By introduction. Under mandate.

## What we work on

Nine specialist disciplines, composed under an orchestrator that routes by task:

| Discipline           | What it does                                                              |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reserving            | Chain-ladder, Mack and bootstrap reserves; ranged, audited, reconciled    |
| Mortality & Life     | Lee-Carter and CBD projections fitted to the book                         |
| Capital & ESG        | ESG paths and SCR under Solvency II / SAM                                 |
| Climate & Cat        | EP curves, parametric triggers, hazard layered onto exposure              |
| Pricing & ML         | GLM and gradient-boosted models with SHAP attribution                     |
| Pensions & Longevity | Projected-unit valuations and longevity scenarios                         |
| Regulatory           | Citation graph from rule to figure; FSCA, PA, IFRS 17, POPIA              |
| Documentation        | Reproducible technical memos and ADR-style decision records               |
| Orchestrator         | Decomposes the business question into the agents and citations it needs   |

## How we publish

**Research, in the open.** Preprints, working papers, reproducible simulators. The W(M, T, R) survival simulator runs entirely in the browser; the SoftData intake desk is the actuary's first thirty minutes, audited.

**Deployment, by mandate.** No public binaries. No public pricing. Engagements with tier-1 insurers, reinsurers, sovereign funds and pension boards, deployed inside the client perimeter under a Master Engagement Agreement.

Every figure traces to its input, the model that produced it, and the regulation it sits under. Defensible to the board. Auditable to the regulator.
