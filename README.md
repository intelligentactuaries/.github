An open-source AI lab for actuarial science.


## What we work on

Reserving, mortality, pensions, pricing, capital adequacy, regulatory compliance, climate risk, and the methodologies that connect them. Wherever traditional actuarial work meets modern AI tooling, we build, test, and publish.

## What we believe

**Domain models matter more than foundation models.** A 200M-parameter foundation model produces interesting forecasts; a peer-reviewed Lee-Carter projection withstands a regulator's scrutiny. We use foundation models as baselines, never as primary methodology.

**Audit-traceability is non-negotiable.** Every numerical output in our work is reproducible from inputs, every methodology decision is documented in an Architecture Decision Record, and every claim is anchored to a pinned regression test that fails if the math drifts.

**Honest findings beat comfortable ones.** Our research publishes what the math says — including when results contradict our prior intuitions. A simulation tuned to confirm a thesis is worth less than nothing; a simulation honoring an unexpected finding is the basis for real understanding.

**Open by default.** Code under permissive licenses, methodology in public ADRs, results reproducible by anyone with the repository. Closed work has its place; ours is to do the public version.

## Where we are

South Africa, with a research lens informed by the realities of developing-world actuarial practice — high inequality, regulatory frameworks under construction, demographic transition, climate exposure. We build tools that work in the contexts where actuarial work matters most.
